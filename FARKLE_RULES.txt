FARKLE RULES
Farkle is a dice game that is multi-player with a minimum of two players, but no upper limit on the number of participants.
Each player will roll 6 dice in a round and may continue base on a few rules. 
The goal is to reach 10,000 points first. 

POINTS
A dice roll of 1 is worth 100 points.
A dice roll of 5 is worth 50 points.
Three of a kind/Three dice with the same face value is worth 100x that face value: three 3's is 300 points and three 5's is 500.
An exception is when three 1's is rolled, it is 1000 not 100.

RULES OF PLAYING
The player rolls 6 dice at same time.
The player has the option to put any point dice away to the side, being 1's, 5's or three of a kind,
Then the player decides if they want to roll again or keep their score. 
A Farkle is when 6 dice or remaining diced rolled and no points appear.
The player will lose all their points for the turn and pass next to the payer.
The player has a chance to not risk a Farkle and keep their points after roll and pass.
If a player rolls 6 dice and they all point dice, the player must rolled again before keeping their points and passing.

ENTERING THE GAME
For players to enter the game, they must rolled until 1,000 points is recorded.
Once the player reach 1,000, now they are able to pass turn in few future turns.   

WIN CONDITIONS
To win in Farkle, a player must reach 10,000 points. 
After a player reaches 10,000, every other player has a turn left to beat that score.
If none are able to beat the player who has the highest, they are crown the winner.

TRICKS
When rolling dice, if you score 1 and 5, it might be a strategy to reroll the 5 for a better chance at scoring.
